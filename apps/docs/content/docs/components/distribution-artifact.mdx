Distribution Artifact pairs a histogram with the statistics needed to
interpret it. The agent supplies validated bins and summary values; the
renderer never derives a statistical conclusion from untrusted prose.

## Selection rule

Use it for the shape of individual numeric observations. Stable bins, ordered
quartiles, count, mean, and median must come from a trusted statistical tool.
Selecting a bar emits `distribution-bin-select` with its bounds.
